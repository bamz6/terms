Personal Standards & Expectations

Clear communication, mutual respect, and consistent effort form the foundation of every relationship.

These are the non-negotiables that govern how I engage in romantic, friendship, and professional relationships.

If these standards do not align with how you operate, it is best we do not proceed.

Communication

  • Consistent, timely communication is expected. Prolonged silence of three or more days without prior notice ends the connection. Being “busy” is not a standing excuse.
  • Effort must be mutual. If one person is initiating the vast majority of contact, the imbalance will be addressed once and then ended if it continues.
  • Vague statements such as “I’ll be free later” without a specific time or follow-through are treated as non-commitments. Either propose a concrete plan or refrain from suggesting availability.

Respect & Reliability

  • Canceling plans with less than 24 hours’ notice without a legitimate reason counts as a strike. Two strikes result in permanent disconnection.
  • Words will not be twisted, gaslit, or used to make someone feel unreasonable for maintaining clear standards.
  • Transparency about other dating or social activity is required where relevant. Ambiguity is not acceptable.

Honesty & Intent

  • Any lie, including small ones, results in an immediate and permanent end to contact. Trust is the baseline requirement, not something earned later.
  • Expressing interest or closeness followed by disappearance or inconsistent behavior is treated as attention-seeking, not genuine connection.
  • Contact limited primarily to late-night hours, moments of boredom, or convenience signals low priority and will not be reciprocated long-term.

Friendships

  • Friendship requires reciprocity. One-sided emotional labor, constant venting without mutual support, or only reaching out when in need will not be sustained.
  • Reliability applies equally to friends. Repeated last-minute cancellations, flaking, or treating plans as optional erode the relationship.
  • Gossip, triangulation, or using me as a sounding board while undermining others will not be tolerated.
  • Boundaries around time, energy, and personal topics are to be respected. Pushing past clearly stated limits ends the friendship.
  • Growth and honesty are valued. Stagnant or purely transactional friendships that offer no mutual enrichment will naturally fall away.

Professional Relationships

  • Professional interactions require clarity, respect, and follow-through. Missed deadlines, ignored messages, or last-minute changes without communication damage trust.
  • Credit and accountability matter. Taking credit for shared work, shifting blame, or failing to acknowledge contributions is unacceptable.
  • Boundaries between personal and professional spheres will be maintained. Over-familiarity, inappropriate comments, or blurring lines without consent will be addressed directly and may end the collaboration.
  • Communication should be efficient and respectful of time. Unnecessary meetings, vague expectations, or chronic unresponsiveness will not be accommodated indefinitely.
  • Integrity is non-negotiable. Dishonesty in professional contexts, including misrepresentation of capabilities or commitments, results in immediate disengagement.

Additional Standards

  • Emotional availability and consistency are required across all relationship types. If you are not ready or willing to invest real time and attention, do not initiate or continue.
  • Consistency matters more than intensity. Sporadic high-effort gestures do not compensate for ongoing unreliability.
  • Boundaries are not up for debate once clearly stated. Pushing against them after they have been communicated ends the interaction.
  • Reciprocity is non-negotiable. If effort, planning, support, or investment is one-sided, the connection will be closed without further discussion.
  • Clarity over convenience. Direct, adult communication is preferred over hints, mixed signals, or passive behavior.

Bottom Line

I do not chase, wait indefinitely, or decode mixed messages in any relationship—romantic, friendship, or professional. Effort that has to be requested is not genuine effort. These standards exist to protect time, energy, and emotional bandwidth. Alignment is welcomed; misalignment is simply filtered out.

These standards apply consistently across all forms of connection.

By agreeing, you confirm you have read these standards and accept them.
